Convoy · UI redesign concepts

One design language across the app: deep-navy “calm navigation” theme, Inter typography, real icons instead of emoji, map-first layout with a single bottom sheet. Green is reserved for you, teal for the route, member colors for identity. Six key screens below.

Screen 1Home — start or join

Convoy
Your group. One map. Nobody left behind.
🔒 You control who sees you ✓ No app install needed

Two intents, two buttons — no form dump on first load. The privacy promise is on screen one because trust is the product. Name/transport are asked contextually later.

QR is the killer invite for in-person meetups. The consent preview turns your visibility rules into a trust feature — joiners know exactly what they’re sharing, with whom, before they tap join.
